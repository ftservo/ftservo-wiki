# HD series specifications

The HD series targets Microduck and Open Duck-style lightweight bipeds and compact multi-joint mechanisms. Its TTL serial bus supports designs that balance actuator size, mass, and joint output.

[Back to specifications catalog](index.md) · [Product selection](../index.md)

!!! info "Series characteristics"
    The compact body, coreless motor, metal gears, and dual-shaft output suit legs, necks, and lightweight grippers. Open-source robot integrations must match mechanics, power, bus protocol, and control parameters together.
